Voice capture and read-aloud use the browser's own free speech APIs, an honest stand-in for Amazon Transcribe and Polly. Price-board photos are read with Tesseract.js, an open-source OCR engine executing the recognition entirely client-side. The price trend above is a genuine linear-regression model computed live from crowd reports, a lightweight stand-in for the Prophet-based forecaster planned for production. Baseline prices are calibrated to real Tamil Nadu wholesale vegetable rates, and wherever a live Agmarknet-linked market report existed for a specific crop at Thanjavur, Kumbakonam, or Mannargudi (dated 19 Jul 2026), that real per-market number replaces the state-level estimate; the rest fall back to a disclosed illustrative adjustment. None of it is randomly generated. Live crowd reports still come from whoever uses this demo. A direct data.gov.in Agmarknet feed and crop identification from a photograph of the produce itself remain on the roadmap.
