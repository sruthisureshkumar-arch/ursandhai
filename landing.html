An offer only a neighbour can verify

A farmer standing in front of a buyer rarely knows whether the price on the table is fair. Government mandi prices arrive a day late and describe an entire district, not the specific village, the specific hour, or the specific buyer standing in front of them right now. Ur Sandhai closes that gap with the people already closest to it: nearby farmers, reporting in real time.

Built for the moment of the sale

Three mechanics, all running live in the working prototype, aimed at the instant a farmer decides whether to sell.

Fair Offer Check

Enter the price a buyer just offered and get an instant, bilingual verdict against the live local average.

Sell Together

When enough neighbours report the same crop in real surplus, the app surfaces a prompt to coordinate a shared sale.

Live Price Trend

A genuine regression, computed on the spot from crowd reports, showing whether prices are rising or falling.
